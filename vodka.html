Origin Story

1405 • Polish Gorzałka

Medicinal Spirit Origins

First written mention of vodka in Sandomierz court documents in Poland used for herbs and medicine.

1894 • Mendeleev Formula

Standard 40% ABV Proof

Russian imperial standard for vodka distillation established at 40% ABV by weight and volume.

1970s • Modern Rectification

Multi-Column Fractional Stills

Introduction of multi-column distillation towers removing volatile aldehydes to reach 96% raw purity.
