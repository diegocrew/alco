ORIGIN STORY

The Vodka Belt & Filtration Masters

1400s • Monastic Stills

Aqua Vitae of the North

First documented in Polish court records and Moscow monasteries as medicinal spirits distilled from local grain mash.

1879 • Continuous Rectification

Lars Olsson Smith

Pioneered fractional column distillation in Sweden, removing fusel oils to create 'Absolut Rent Brännvin'.

Modern • Filtration Arts

Birch Charcoal & Freeze Filtration

Filtered through quartz sand, silver threads, and active birch charcoal to achieve crystal clarity while preserving subtle pepper or cream texture.
