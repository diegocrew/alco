ORIGIN STORY

From Sugar Cane to Liquid Gold

1640s • Caribbean Origins

Plantation Distillation

First distilled on Caribbean sugarcane plantations when enslaved workers discovered molasses—a byproduct of sugar refining—could be fermented into alcohol.

1655 • Royal Navy Tot

The Navy Ration

When Britain captured Jamaica, the Royal Navy replaced daily French brandy rations with rum. The daily 'tot' at 54.5% ABV continued until Black Tot Day in 1970.

1800s • Wooden Stills

Demerara & Pot Stills

British Guiana developed unique greenheart wooden pot and Coffey stills along the Demerara river, creating unmatched heavy, dark, aromatic spirit profiles.

1996 • AOC Protection

French Agricole AOC

Martinique earned the prestigious Appellation d'Origine Contrôlée (AOC) for Rhum Agricole, regulating fresh sugarcane juice distillation like Fine Cognac.
