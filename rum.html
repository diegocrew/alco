Origin Story

1640s • Caribbean Origins

Plantation Distillation

First distilled on Caribbean sugarcane plantations when molasses was discovered to be fermentable into high spirit.

1655 • Royal Navy Tot

The Navy Ration

When Britain captured Jamaica, daily French brandy rations were replaced with rum at 54.5% ABV proof.

1800s • Wooden Stills

Demerara & Pot Stills

British Guiana developed unique greenheart wooden pot stills creating heavy aromatic spirit profiles.

1996 • AOC Protection

French Agricole AOC

Martinique earned Appellation d'Origine Contrôlée (AOC) protection for fresh sugarcane juice Rhum Agricole.
