THE FIELD BOARD

The Green Fairy, Banned and Reborn

Origin Story

1792 • Val-de-Travers

A Swiss Herbal Tonic

Absinthe begins as a medicinal elixir in the Val-de-Travers, commercialised by Major Dubied and his son-in-law Henri-Louis Pernod.

1860s • L'Heure Verte

The Green Hour

Phylloxera devastated the vineyards and made wine expensive. Absinthe filled Parisian cafés, and five o'clock became the green hour.

1915 → 2007 • Ban & Revival

Thujone on Trial

Blamed for madness after the Lanfray murders, absinthe was banned across Europe and the US. Modern analysis exonerated thujone, and legal sales resumed.
