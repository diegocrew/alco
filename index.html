FOUNDATIONS

The Anatomy of Barrel Aging & Distillation

01. Fermentation

Yeast & Sugars

Whether starting from sugarcane juice, molasses, malted barley, or crushed grapes, fermentation converts raw fermentable sugars into ethanol and aromatic congeners.

02. Distillation

Pot vs. Column

Pot stills capture rich, heavy essential oils and esters in small batches, while continuous multi-column stills yield crisp, highly rectified clean spirits.

03. Maturation

Wood & Climate

Barrels breathe with temperature fluctuations. Tropical maturation accelerates flavor extraction up to three times faster than temperate European cellars.
