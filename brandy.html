1700s • Spanish Solera

Brandy de Jerez

Matured spirit through cascading tier casks previously holding fragrant Oloroso and Pedro Ximénez sherry.

1553 • Normandy Cider

Gilles de Gouberville

First recorded distillation of cider into apple eau-de-vie in Normandy: the foundation of Calvados.
