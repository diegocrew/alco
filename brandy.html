Origin Story

1310 • Vital Du Four

Armagnac Medical Treatise

Cardinal Vital Du Four wrote 40 virtues of Armagnac in Gascony, predating Cognac by over 150 years.

1700s • Spanish Solera System

Brandy de Jerez

Andalusia perfected aging wine spirits in seasoned Sherry oak casks utilizing dynamic Solera criaderas.

1900s • Pisco & Calvados

Fruit Eau-de-Vie Terroirs

Regional designations for Normandy apple cider brandy (Calvados) and South American grape spirits (Pisco).
