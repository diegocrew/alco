Origin Story

6000 BCE • Cradle of Wine

Ancient Qvevri Fermentation

Earliest evidence of wild grape vinification discovered in subterranean clay amphorae in Georgia.

1855 • Bordeaux Classification

Napoleon III Ranking

Established the historic 1855 classification ranking Left Bank Bordeaux châteaux into five Grand Cru classes.

1976 • Judgment of Paris

Blind Tasting Revolution

California Cabernet Sauvignon and Chardonnay defeated top French Grand Crus in blind Paris tasting.

Signature Serve

Sangría

Pitcher • Macerated • Served Over Ice

  • 750 mlYoung Spanish red
  • 60 mlBrandy de Jerez
  • 30 mlOrange liqueur
  • to topSoda water

Garnish: Sliced orange, lemon & apple

Sangría began as a way to stretch rough wine through a hot Iberian summer. Let the fruit macerate for a few hours before the soda goes in.
