Origin Story

1494 • Scottish Exchequer Rolls

Friar John Cor Malt Order

First official written record of scotch distillation ordering eight bolls of malt for Friar John Cor.

1923 • Suntory Yamazaki

Japanese Whisky Birth

Shinjiro Torii and Masataka Taketsuru established Japan's first commercial malt whisky distillery in Yamazaki.

1964 • Federal Bourbon Standard

America's Native Spirit

US Congress declared Bourbon Whiskey a distinctive product of the United States requiring 51% corn and new charred oak barrels.
