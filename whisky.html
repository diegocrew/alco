ORIGIN STORY

Oak, Peat & Malt Traditions

1494 • Aqua Vitae

Exchequer Rolls

First Scottish record granting Friar John Cor 8 bolls of malt to make aqua vitae by order of the King.

1823 • Excise Act

Legal Commercial Distilling

Paved the way for licensed Highland and Speyside distilleries to construct iconic swan neck copper pot stills.

1923 • Japanese Pioneer

Shinjiro Torii & Masataka Taketsuru

Established Yamazaki outside Kyoto, fusing Scottish pot still distilling with Japanese climate and Mizunara oak.
