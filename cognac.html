ORIGIN STORY

Grande Champagne & Charentais Stills

1600s • Dutch Trade

Brandewijn Transport

Dutch merchants distilled Charente wine to preserve it during sea transport, creating 'brandewijn' (burnt wine).

1700s • Charentais Alembics

Double Pot Still Distillation

Perfected copper pot still double distillation (brouillis and bonne chauffe) preserving grape aromas.

1909 • AOC Cru Boundaries

Grande Champagne & Borderies

Established legal AOC crus protecting Grande Champagne, Petite Champagne, and Borderies chalk soils.
