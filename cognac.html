THE FIELD BOARD

Charentais Double Distillation

Origin Story

1630s • Dutch Merchants

Burnt Wine (Brandwijn)

Dutch traders distilled low-alcohol Charente wine to preserve it for sea transport back to Northern Europe.

1700s • Charentais Pot Still

Double Distillation Art

Perfected the two-stage copper alembic distillation (Repasse) capturing delicate grape esters and rancio aromas.

1909 • Six Cru Regions

Cru Terroir Boundaries

French decree defined the 6 Cognac crus: Grande Champagne, Petite Champagne, Borderies, Fins Bois, Bons Bois, and Bois Ordinaires.

Signature Cocktail

Sidecar

Coupe Glass • Shaken • Sugar Rim

  • 50 mlVSOP cognac
  • 20 mlCointreau
  • 20 mlFresh lemon juice

Garnish: Sugared rim, half the glass only

Claimed by both the Ritz in Paris and Buck's Club in London, and named for the motorcycle sidecar its inventor supposedly arrived in.
