HOUSE RULES

Four Things That Fix Most Drinks

01. Citrus

Squeeze It Today

Lemon and lime juice start losing their edge within a few hours and taste flat by the next day. Nothing else in a sour matters as much.

02. Ice

More, Not Less

A glass packed with ice dilutes more slowly than a half-empty one, because the drink stays colder. Small wet cubes are the enemy.

03. Dilution

Water Is an Ingredient

Shaking or stirring adds roughly 20 to 25 percent water. A drink that tastes harsh is usually under-shaken rather than badly balanced.

04. Vermouth

It Is Wine

Once opened, vermouth belongs in the fridge and should be replaced within a month. A dusty bottle has ruined more Martinis than bad gin ever has.
