Origin Story

1500s • Pulque & Spanish Stills

Mezcal Wine of Tequila

Spanish conquerors introduced copper stills to ferment roasted agave piñas previously used for indigenous pulque.

1758 • Royal Distilling License

Jose Cuervo Patent

King Ferdinand VI of Spain granted the first official royal land patent to cultivate Blue Weber agave in Tequila town.

1974 • Tequila Denomination

Denominación de Origen

Secured global legal protection restricting Tequila production exclusively to Jalisco and selected surrounding municipalities.
