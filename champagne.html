ORIGIN STORY

Effervescence & Méthode Champenoise

1668 • Abbey of Hautvillers

Dom Pierre Pérignon

Benedictine monk who pioneered blending grape crus, white wine from dark grapes, and cork stoppers tied with string.

1818 • Madame Clicquot

The Riddling Table

Veuve Clicquot invented the riddling table to clarify sparkling champagne by collecting yeast sediment in the neck.

1936 • AOC Champagne

Legal Region Protection

Enforced strict Méthode Champenoise rules reserving the Champagne name exclusively to wines grown in the Champagne AOC.
